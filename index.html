FAQ

Is this insurance?

No. It's a rebate program funded by a pool. Payouts depend on the pool balance, and protection pauses if it runs low.

Do I keep the card?

Yes. The rebate comes on top of the card you pulled.

Which card value counts?

The insured value the marketplace records at the moment of the pull.

Can the fee cost more than I get back?

Yes, on small shortfalls. The numbers above include the fee so you see it before you pay.

Why the lowest balance?

So tiers reward people who hold, not people who buy right before opening.
