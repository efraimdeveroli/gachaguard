Overview

Gacha Guard adds a rebate to graded-card pack pulls. If the card you pull is worth less than the pack you paid for, part of the difference comes back to your wallet in USDC. You keep the card either way.

It works on top of Collector Crypt packs. Collector Crypt runs the packs, the odds, the vaulting and the buyback. Gacha Guard adds one thing: a protection fee when you open, and a rebate when the pull falls short.

  • You keep the card. A rebate never takes the card or pauses your buyback, sale or shipping options.
  • The fee is visible. A flat 10% on top of the pack price, shown before you confirm.
  • How much comes back depends on $GUARD. Holding $GUARD sets your coverage at 25%, 50% or 100% of the shortfall.
  • Rebates are capped at $250 per pack.

Protection is not a guarantee of profit. You still pay the fee, and a rebate only covers part of a bad pull unless you are in the top tier. See Risks and limits.

How a rebate is calculated

The rebate is a share of the shortfall: how far the card falls below the pack price.

shortfall = pack price − card value (never below 0)
rebate   = coverage × shortfall
paid     = min(rebate, $250)
  • Pack price is the price of the pack before the protection fee. The fee is not part of the shortfall.
  • Card value is the insured value Collector Crypt assigns to the card at the time of the pull. See the integration.
  • Coverage is 25%, 50% or 100%, set by your $GUARD tier.
  • If the card is worth the pack price or more, the shortfall is $0 and there is no rebate. Nothing is taken back.
  • Amounts are rounded to the nearest cent.

The $250 cap

A Master pack ($500) with a card worth $40 leaves a $460 shortfall. At 100% coverage that is $460, but the rebate is capped at $250. At 50% it is $230, under the cap, so you receive $230.

The pull window

The tier that applies to a pull is based on the lowest $GUARD balance your wallet held during the pull window: from the moment you open the pack until your rebate is paid. Buying $GUARD just before you open, then selling straight after, will not raise your tier. If you hold below 0.1% at any point in the window, the pull has no coverage.

The live tier and balance are shown on the site under Your coverage once your wallet is connected.

Collector Crypt integration

Collector Crypt is a Solana platform for tokenized graded cards. A pack pull gives you an NFT that represents a real graded card held in a vault. Gacha Guard reads that pull and pays the rebate. It does not change the pack, the odds or the card.

What Collector Crypt handles

  • Packs and odds. Odds are set by each machine. Gacha Guard does not touch them.
  • The card. Each pull is a graded card held in a vault, represented by a Solana NFT in your wallet.
  • Instant buyback. Collector Crypt offers a buyback on each pull for a limited time, at a rate set per machine and based on the card's insured value.
  • Shipping. You can redeem the card to have it shipped, which burns the NFT. Collector Crypt sets the fees and timing.
  • Payments. Packs are priced in USDC, plus a little SOL for the network fee.

What Gacha Guard handles

1 / Open
You open a protected pack through Gacha Guard. One wallet approval pays the pack price to Collector Crypt and the 10% protection fee to Gacha Guard.
2 / Read
Gacha Guard reads the pull on-chain: which pack, which card, and the card's insured value at pull time.
3 / Tier
Your coverage is set from the lowest $GUARD balance in your pull window.
4 / Rebate
The rebate is calculated from pack price, card value and coverage, then capped at $250.
5 / Pay
You claim it and USDC is sent to the wallet that opened the pack.

Card value is the insured value

Collector Crypt gives every card an insured value, a reference value it uses for buyback. It is not a market price and not an insurance policy. Gacha Guard uses the same number so the rebate and the buyback are measured the same way. The value is taken at the time of the pull. Later changes to the card's value do not change the rebate.

Buyback, selling and shipping

A rebate is worked out from the pull itself. What you do with the card afterwards (instant buyback, sale on a marketplace, transfer or shipping) does not change it. It also does not stop you from doing any of those things.

Gacha Guard is a separate layer. Packs, vaulting, buyback and shipping are Collector Crypt's services and follow Collector Crypt's terms. Questions about a card, a vault or a shipment go to Collector Crypt.

Claiming your rebate

  • Once a pull has settled, the rebate is worked out for you. There is nothing to submit or prove.
  • You claim it with one wallet approval. USDC is sent to the wallet that opened the pack.
  • Rebates are paid from the USDC rebate pool, so payouts depend on what the pool holds. The pool balance is shown live on the site.
  • Pulls that are worth the pack price or more have no rebate, so there is nothing to claim.
  • Every claim shows in the Live rebates feed with the wallet, pack, card and amount.

Where the fee goes

The protection fee is split in two, in the open:

RESERVE50%Goes to the USDC pool that pays rebates.
BURN50%Buys $GUARD on the market and burns it.

The pool pays rebates. The burn reduces the $GUARD supply, which makes each tier's percentage of supply worth a little more over time. The fee is what keeps protection running, so it is charged on every protected pack, whether or not you get a rebate.

Risks and limits

  • You can still lose money. The fee is always paid, and a rebate is only part of the shortfall unless you hold the top tier.
  • The cap is $250. Large shortfalls on Master packs are only partly covered.
  • Payouts come from the pool. If the pool is low, claims are limited by what it holds.
  • Coverage depends on holding $GUARD. If your balance falls below a tier's level during the pull window, the lower tier applies. $GUARD's price can go down as well as up.
  • Insured value is a reference. It may be above or below what a card sells for.
  • Smart contracts and wallets carry risk. Use a wallet you trust and check each approval before you sign.
  • Availability can vary by region and follows Collector Crypt's own restrictions.

Not financial advice. Gacha Guard is a rebate on a game of chance. It is not an investment and not insurance. Only open packs you are comfortable paying for.

FAQ

Do I keep the card?

Yes. The card goes to your wallet like any Collector Crypt pull. The rebate is paid in USDC on top.

Is the fee part of the rebate calculation?

No. The shortfall uses the pack price only: pack price minus card value. The fee is paid on top and is not refunded.

What if I pull a card worth more than the pack?

There is no shortfall and no rebate. Nothing is taken from you or from the card.

What happens if I sell the card back or ship it?

Nothing changes for the rebate. It is worked out from the pull. You can use Collector Crypt's buyback, sell, transfer or ship the card as you normally would.

How is the card value decided?

It is the insured value Collector Crypt shows for the card at the time of the pull, the same number the buyback uses.

Which tier will I get?

It depends on the lowest $GUARD balance your wallet held from the moment you opened the pack until the rebate is paid: 0.1% of supply for Holder, 1% for Collector, 2% for Whale.

Why is the maximum $250?

A cap keeps the pool able to pay many claims. It only affects large shortfalls, mostly on Master packs.

Can I use my own wallet from a hardware device?

Any Solana wallet that can sign the pack approval works. The wallet that opens the pack is the wallet that receives the card and the rebate.

Who runs the packs?

Collector Crypt. Gacha Guard does not run packs, hold cards or set odds.
